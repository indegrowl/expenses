# Case Study: Dave Ramsey-style budgeting categories (zero-based budget)

## Who / source
Ramsey Solutions — guidelines for budget percentages and common category structure.

## The categorization idea
A **zero-based budget** assigns every rupee a job.
Ramsey emphasizes a set of common category “folders”:
- Giving
- Saving
- Food
- Utilities
- Housing
- Transportation
- Health
- Insurance
- Childcare
- Lifestyle/Entertainment
- Personal Spending
- Miscellaneous
- Debt

Even if you don’t follow Ramsey’s philosophy, this is a very “standard consumer budget taxonomy” that many people map to.

## Practical takeaways
- Broad categories are enough to start.
- “Miscellaneous” is useful as a buffer, but if it grows, it’s a smell.

## How this maps to hledger
- This maps cleanly to an `Expenses:*` tree.
- If you want very low maintenance, you can stop at this level and avoid deep subcategories.
